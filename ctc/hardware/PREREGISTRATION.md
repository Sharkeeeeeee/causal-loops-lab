## Pass criteria

- **C1 (the rules are told apart):** for every quantity where past-preserving and P-CTC differ by at least 0.1 (exact), the measured difference has the predicted sign and |z| >= 5.0, with the standard deviation of the difference from a bootstrap that resamples both the shots and the readout calibration.
- **C2 (accuracy):** every past-preserving quantity is within 0.05 of its exact value.
- **C3 (quantum eraser):** under past-preserving, the fringe sorted by Bob X = + has visibility >= 0.85, its shift is within 0.15 rad of the exact value, and its visibility exceeds the no-loop value by z >= 5.0.

## Known limits, stated in advance

- All three rules are computed from the same shots. Passing the criteria shows that each rule's predictions can be measured on hardware; it does not show which rule nature uses, or that loops exist.
- Under past-preserving selection, world weights (including Bob's own statistics) are taken from all shots by construction, so they equal the no-loop values. Their agreement with prediction tests the hardware, not the rule.
- The eraser circuits read Bob in X (eraser_x, eraser_y) or Z (eraser_z). Past-preserving uses Bob's reading as the record, so it uses the X record in the first two and the which-path record in the third. Which record basis nature would use is the open problem in ctc/PAST_PRESERVING.md; this run does not decide it.
- "Should be 0" quantities are limited by two-qubit gate errors, which readout correction does not remove. C2 may fail for them; that outcome will be reported as is.
